## What I worked on

I led the Ruby team building a school social network for parents, teachers and students in the USA. I planned and prioritized features with the client, wrote code and helped the team deliver the work on time.

## Code and team

I reviewed and tested code, refactored legacy parts of the app and worked on performance and maintainability. I also interviewed Ruby developers and supported the team as they worked through features.

Regular conversations with the client helped us understand what users needed and decide what to build next.

## Development workflow

I set up GitFlow, continuous integration and a defined deployment process. Code review became part of the workflow and we used Slack, Telegram and JIRA to coordinate work and keep the team informed.
